# Add the Lion Watermark to Every Public Page

## What will change
- Create one reusable decorative lion watermark matching the About page treatment.
- Add it to the dark opening area on Services, Projects, Partners, Contact, Newsroom, article, and legal pages.
- Keep the Home page unchanged.
- Keep the lion subtle, behind the page text, and hidden on smaller screens so it never crowds content.

## Technical details
- Reuse the existing Litigon lion image rather than duplicate assets.
- Position it consistently at the upper-right of each dark page header with low opacity and no pointer interaction.
- Preserve all existing page copy, navigation, animation, and functionality.
- Verify representative pages at desktop and mobile sizes and confirm the site builds successfully.
